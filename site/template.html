How it works

The goal

Find liquid stocks that can gain 10% or 20% within two to six weeks, so a handful of 20% moves a year can compound a portfolio.

Two tiers

Stocks over $2B with a price over $10 and $20M traded a day are split by average daily range. 4.0% or more is the Double tier. 2.8% up to 4.0% is the Single tier. Range was the one measure that predicted target hits in every year tested.

How trades were tested

Every week from September 2017, each qualifying stock was entered at the close. A trade ended at the target, at the stop, or after 30 trading days. When both traded on one day the stop was assumed first, and gaps through the stop filled at the open.

When to look

Entering on any weekday, or at Monday's open instead of Friday's close, gave about the same results, so a Monday plan and a daily check both rest on the data.

What did not help

Stage, sector stage, the SPY regime, an uptrend filter, base and pullback patterns, volume and a record of big moves all failed to help in both halves of the data.

Limits

Only stocks over $2B today are included, which flatters the absolute results, so rely on comparisons. Costs, taxes and earnings dates are not counted. This is research, not investment advice.
